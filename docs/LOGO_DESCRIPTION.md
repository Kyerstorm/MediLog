# AI Logo Generation Prompts - Health Calendar App

## 🎨 Primary Prompt (Recommended)

Create a modern, minimalist app icon for an Android health management application called "Health Calendar". Design a clean, geometric icon that combines a calendar grid with subtle medical elements. Use bright cyan blue (#4FC3F7) as the primary color with optional purple (#9575CD) gradient accents. The icon should feature rounded corners, simple shapes, and work perfectly at small sizes (72dp-512px). Style: Material Design 3, professional yet friendly, trustworthy and calming. The icon must work on both light and dark backgrounds. Avoid clinical hospital imagery, needles, or scary medical symbols. Target: tablet-optimized health app for medication tracking and appointment management.

---

## 🔄 Alternative Prompts

### Prompt 1: Calendar + Pill Integration
Modern app icon featuring a simplified calendar grid with a stylized pill capsule integrated into one of the date squares. Use bright cyan blue (#4FC3F7) for the calendar with purple (#9575CD) gradient on the pill. Clean lines, rounded corners, Material Design 3 style. Minimalist, professional, recognizable at small sizes. No text, just geometric shapes.

### Prompt 2: Time + Health Monogram
Abstract lettermark icon combining "H" and "C" letterforms that subtly incorporate a clock face and heart symbol. Primary color: bright cyan (#4FC3F7), with purple (#9575CD) accent. Rounded geometric shapes, modern sans-serif influence, minimalist design. Should feel trustworthy and organized, not clinical.

### Prompt 3: Shield + Calendar
App icon showing a rounded shield shape containing a simplified 3x3 calendar grid with a small medical cross or heart in the center square. Colors: cyan blue (#4FC3F7) to purple (#9575CD) gradient. Clean vector style, Material Design 3 aesthetic, rounded corners throughout. Professional health app feel.

### Prompt 4: Circular Calendar Clock
Circular app icon combining clock face with calendar elements - numbers around the edge representing dates, with pill/capsule shapes as clock hands. Bright cyan blue (#4FC3F7) primary, purple (#9575CD) accents. Modern, geometric, minimalist. High contrast for visibility at small sizes.

### Prompt 5: Document + Health
Minimalist icon showing a simplified document/paper sheet with a subtle medical plus symbol or pill icon overlay. Color gradient from cyan (#4FC3F7) to purple (#9575CD). Rounded corners, clean lines, Material Design 3 style. Professional medical app aesthetic without being clinical.

## 🎯 Design Specifications

**Colors:**
- Primary: #4FC3F7 (bright cyan blue)
- Secondary: #9575CD (medium purple)
- Background: Transparent or solid color layer

**Style Keywords:**
- Modern, minimalist, clean, geometric
- Material Design 3
- Rounded corners, soft edges
- Professional yet friendly
- High contrast for small sizes
- No gradients (or subtle gradients only)

**Must Include:**
- Rounded corners throughout
- Simple, recognizable shapes
- Works at 72dp to 512px
- Legible on light AND dark backgrounds

**Must Avoid:**
- Clinical hospital imagery
- Syringes, needles, blood
- Pharmacy green crosses
- Red colors (too alarming)
- Cluttered or complex designs
- Text/typography
- Realistic illustrations

---

## 📐 Technical Requirements

**Format:** Vector icon, square canvas (1:1 ratio)
**Size:** 512px × 512px minimum
**Safe Zone:** Keep main elements within center 66% circle
**Style:** Flat design or subtle gradient only
**Transparency:** Yes (for adaptive icon)
