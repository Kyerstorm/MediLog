# Health Calendar App - Logo Design Brief

## App Overview
**Health Calendar** is a comprehensive Android health management application that helps users track medications, manage medical appointments, and organize health documents. The app is designed specifically for foldable tablets and large-screen devices, emphasizing a clean, modern, and accessible interface.

## Core Features
- **Medication Management**: Track prescriptions, dosages, schedules, and reminders
- **Appointment Calendar**: Schedule and manage medical appointments with notifications
- **Document Scanner**: Scan and organize medical documents, prescriptions, and lab results
- **NHS Integration**: Search UK NHS medication database for drug information
- **Alarm System**: Smart medication reminders and appointment alerts
- **Notes System**: Track medical notes and health observations

## Target Audience
- Adults managing chronic conditions or multiple medications
- Elderly users requiring medication adherence support
- Caregivers tracking health information for family members
- Anyone needing organized health record management

## Design Philosophy
- **Material Design 3**: Modern, clean UI following Google's latest design guidelines
- **Accessibility First**: High contrast modes, AMOLED dark theme, readable typography
- **Tablet Optimized**: Responsive layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, and foldable devices
- **Calming & Professional**: Health-focused color scheme that inspires trust and calm

## Color Palette
- **Primary Color**: Medication Blue (#4FC3F7) - Bright, trustworthy cyan/light blue
- **Secondary Color**: Medication Purple (#9575CD) - Calming, sophisticated medium purple
- **Accent**: Pink/Rose tones for highlights
- **Themes**: 
  - Light mode with clean whites and soft backgrounds
  - Dark mode with deep grays
  - AMOLED mode with pure blacks for battery efficiency

## Logo Requirements

### Style Preferences
- **Modern & Minimalist**: Clean lines, not cluttered
- **Professional yet Friendly**: Medical/health-focused but approachable, not clinical
- **Recognizable at Small Sizes**: Must work as an app icon (72dp to 512px)
- **Versatile**: Works on light backgrounds, dark backgrounds, and transparent

### Visual Elements to Consider
- **Calendar/Date**: Representing appointment scheduling and daily tracking
- **Medical Cross/Plus**: Universal health symbol (optional, avoid being too clinical)
- **Pills/Medication**: Capsule or pill shapes (subtle, not pharmacy-style)
- **Heart**: Health, care, wellness (optional accent)
- **Clock/Time**: Reminders, schedules, medication timing
- **Shield**: Protection, health management, reliability

### Suggested Concepts
1. **Calendar + Health**: A calendar icon with a medical element (pill, cross, heart) integrated
2. **Stylized 'HC' Monogram**: Abstract letterforms incorporating health imagery
3. **Time + Medicine**: Clock face with pill/capsule hands
4. **Calendar Grid with Heart**: Calendar squares forming or containing a heart
5. **Document + Medical**: Paper/document with health cross or pill icon

### Colors for Logo
- Use the app's primary blue (#4FC3F7) as the main color
- Optional secondary purple (#9575CD) for accents or gradients
- Consider a gradient from blue to purple for depth
- Ensure it works in:
  - Full color (for marketing)
  - Single color (for monochrome contexts)
  - White/light version (for dark backgrounds)
  - Dark version (for light backgrounds)

### Typography (if text included)
- **Font Style**: Sans-serif, modern, rounded, friendly
- **Weight**: Medium to bold for readability
- **Avoid**: Serif fonts, overly medical/clinical typefaces, script fonts

### Shape Language
- **Rounded corners**: Friendly, modern, accessible
- **Circular elements**: Wholeness, health, cycles (medication schedules)
- **Grid patterns**: Calendar structure, organization
- **Avoid**: Sharp angles, aggressive shapes, chaotic elements

## Icon Specifications (Android)
- **Adaptive Icon**: Foreground and background layers for Android 8.0+
- **Sizes Needed**: 
  - 48dp (mdpi)
  - 72dp (hdpi)
  - 96dp (xhdpi)
  - 144dp (xxhdpi)
  - 192dp (xxxhdpi)
  - 512px (Play Store)
- **Safe Zone**: Keep important elements within center 66% circle (adaptive icon crop)
- **Format**: PNG with transparency, or vector (SVG)

## Mood & Feeling
- **Trustworthy**: Users are managing their health - inspire confidence
- **Calm**: Health management can be stressful - provide peace of mind
- **Organized**: Clear, structured, not chaotic
- **Modern**: Current, up-to-date, technologically capable
- **Caring**: Supportive, helpful, not cold or distant
- **Empowering**: User is in control of their health journey

## What to Avoid
- ❌ Overly clinical/hospital imagery (syringes, stethoscopes, hospital crosses)
- ❌ Scary medical symbols (needles, blood, surgery tools)
- ❌ Pharmaceutical branding aesthetics (green crosses, pharmacy logos)
- ❌ Cluttered designs with too many elements
- ❌ Overly feminine or masculine imagery (app is for everyone)
- ❌ Childish or cartoon-style illustrations
- ❌ Religious symbols (medical cross should be secular)
- ❌ Red color (too alarming, associated with emergencies/errors)

## Competitors for Reference (Differentiate From)
- **Medisafe**: Blue pill bottle logo
- **MyTherapy**: Red/pink medicine bottle
- **Pill Reminder**: Simple pill icons
- **Round Health**: Circular clock-based design

**Goal**: Create something that feels more sophisticated, modern, and tablet-optimized than competitors while maintaining the health/medication focus.

## Usage Context
The logo will appear:
- Android home screens and app drawers
- Play Store listing (main icon, feature graphics)
- App splash screen on launch
- About page within the app
- Notification icons (simplified version)
- Marketing materials (website, screenshots, promotional content)

## Deliverable Preferences
- Vector format (SVG) for scalability
- High-resolution PNG with transparency (at least 512px)
- Adaptive icon layers (foreground + background)
- Monochrome/single-color version
- Color variations (light mode, dark mode, AMOLED)
